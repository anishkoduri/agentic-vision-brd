Pen 6 — Behavioral Overview

24,327 track-minutes from 1,749 clips (Day 1: 868 clips, Day 2: 881). Unit: one tracked box in one minute.

What this page is — and is not

Herd-level evidence only. Tracker IDs are per clip and never carry across clips, so no animal is followed between days, no individual is identified, and nothing here ranks cows or diagnoses disease. Labels are geometric rules on bounding boxes.
